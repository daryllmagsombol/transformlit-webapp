## Global Constraints

### Scope and product decisions

- Download **selected individual Bible chapters** and **whole Transformlit books** only.
- Bible offline reading is **read-only**. Bible position is a local navigation preference, not a synchronized mutation.
- Book mutations are limited to:
  - Reading progress.
  - Bookmark add/remove.
  - Highlight/note create, read, update, and delete. Notes remain part of highlights.
- Do not add bookmark label/color editing, Bible mutations, offline social features, audio downloads, general change feeds, CRDTs, or event sourcing.
- Preserve conflicts and deleted-record history. Never silently overwrite an offline edit.
- Downloads remain available until explicit removal or sign-out. Do not add expiry or automatic access-revocation eviction.
- Removing content must not remove pending edits. Old-version annotations remain anchored to their original content version or become visibly unresolved.

### Persistence, authentication, and privacy

- IndexedDB is the persistence authority for private offline content, reader records, receipts, outbox operations, and lifecycle state.
- Zustand retains transient state and presentation preferences; it must not independently persist authoritative book progress.
- Partition every private record by immutable authenticated subject, not email or display name.
- Establish local account ownership only after authenticated identity verification. A persisted display profile alone is insufficient.
- Offline account access is distinct from current server authentication.
- Never persist access tokens or refresh tokens.
- Network failures and server outages pause authentication-dependent work without deleting local data.
- Genuine authentication failure requires same-account reauthentication before replay.
- Sign-out and account switching require successful drain or explicit discard, a durable barrier, old-account cleanup, and serialized remote logout.
- Offline/failed remote logout leaves a durable deferred-logout marker and blocks login/account activation until old-session invalidation completes.
- Installed PWAs and ordinary tabs share origin storage. Coordinate lifecycle transitions, refresh, and replay across tabs.
- Do not claim account namespacing protects against same-origin XSS or an unlocked browser.

### Service worker and deployment

- No authenticated HTML, GraphQL, auth, reading-session/page responses, downloads, or RSC payloads in Cache Storage.
- HTML fallback is permitted only for eligible **document navigation** requests. Never return fallback HTML for RSC/data requests.
- No authenticated replay or account storage in worker code.
- No unconditional `skipWaiting`, forced reload, or IndexedDB deletion during worker activation.
- `/sw.js` must be same-origin, root-scoped, JavaScript, and served with:
  - `Cache-Control: no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate`
  - CSP containing `worker-src 'self'`.
- Retain assets needed by supported old workers/tabs through rollout.
- IndexedDB upgrades are transactional and non-destructive. Unsupported older clients must fail safely rather than delete or downgrade data.
